# Code of Conduct

Trequila follows the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).
In short: be respectful, assume good faith, keep discussion on the work, and do not harass anyone.

## Reporting

To report unacceptable behaviour, open a private security advisory titled "Conduct report" at
<https://github.com/Sky-walkerX/Trequila/security/advisories/new>, which only the maintainers can read.
Reports are reviewed promptly and handled confidentially.

Maintainers may remove comments, commits or contributions that violate this code, and may restrict
participation by anyone who does.
